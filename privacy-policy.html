NovaStack Privacy Policy

Updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

NovaStack is a puzzle game made by Oula Chrish ("we"). Contact: gab00012c@gmail.com.

What the game stores

Your progress (scores, coins, items, settings, your player name and avatar) is stored only on your phone. We run no servers and have no accounts: we never receive this data.

Ads

NovaStack shows only reward videos that you choose to watch. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EU, UK and other places that require it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy choices. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

NovaStack Plus and coin packs are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we only receive a confirmation that the purchase happened, never your payment details.

Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. It sends nothing anywhere. Turn it off in Settings > Daily reminder.

Children

NovaStack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset statistics / Reset settings, or uninstall the app: that removes everything it stored. To reset your advertising ID, use your phone's Google settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
